# Hydrofrac: design retrospective

Hydrofrac is lineage-driven fault injection for Hydro programs in the manner of Molly (Alvaro, Rosen, Hellerstein, SIGMOD 2015): run the program in the simulator, derive why its outputs held, and search for a fault set the program's own transport declarations admit under which a correct member lacks a fact another holds. After six rounds the design converged on two lineage levels (`Level::Execution` and `Level::Precise`), a sender-side fault point on every `Network` send, the transport type as the fault model, a fault universe grown lazily from the derivations the search has seen, and a specification generated from the program's `EventualConsistency` labels. This document records what the experiments taught, which approaches were tried and closed and why, which questions are now settled, and what remains open, for whoever continues the work. The user guide is [hydrofrac.md](hydrofrac.md); the worked examples are [hydrofrac-ec-corpus.md](hydrofrac-ec-corpus.md).

## What the experiments taught

### Provenance granularity

Four levels were measured on the same searches: `Execution` (L0, every output of an execution depends on every input of it and of earlier executions at its node), `Slice` (L1, L0 narrowed by a static backward slice), `Record` (L2, provenance tags from the `with_provenance()` rewrite), and `Counterfactual` (L3, node-local replay removing deliveries one at a time). L0 equalled L1 in every cell of every protocol. L2 paid exactly where there was background load or redundancy: on 2PC with cooperative termination at `n=4 load=8`, 101 hypotheses became 23, and the count is load-invariant at L2 ([ablation](ldfi-level-ablation.md#2pc-with-cooperative-termination-ctptrue-the-certificate)). L3 reproduced L2 wherever L2 applied, at two to ten times the cost ([recommendation record](ldfi-level-ablation.md#recommendation-record)). Raft, one `raft_step` closure per tick, is the exception: L0 certifies in 59 hypotheses where L2 needs 107 and seven times the wall time, so `Execution` survives for closure-encoded programs ([Raft](ldfi-level-ablation.md#raft)). Commit `4ddea33f19` collapsed to two levels; `fafcd70fe3` made `Precise` mean record provenance where present and replay everywhere else ([Precise without provenance](ldfi-level-ablation.md#precise-without-provenance)).

### The soundness bug the ablation caught

The first ISR grid showed L2 finding the unclean-election bug while L0, L1 and L3 certified it. The cause was not vacuous hypotheses but two clause-soundness gaps ([soundness bug](ldfi-level-ablation.md#soundness-bug-two-lineage-gaps-not-vacuous-hypotheses), commit `4c43956b4b`). First, `Lineage::crash_ordinal` clamped a dependency's send ordinal at the sender's pre-`EFF` count while the simulator fired crash points anywhere, so `killed_by` judged a post-`EFF` acknowledgement's crash to kill nothing ([root cause 1](ldfi-level-ablation.md#root-cause-1-l0-l1-dependency-ordinals-clamped-at-eff)). Second, `Spec::observe_sequenced` prefixed the payload with a `usize` that `payload_matches` never recognised, so sequenced views were untraced facts at L3 ([root cause 2](ldfi-level-ablation.md#root-cause-2-l3-sequenced-observations-were-never-traced-to-their-sends)). After the fix every level agrees in every cell, pinned by `ldfi_finds_the_unclean_election_bug_at_every_level` ([after the fix](ldfi-level-ablation.md#after-the-fix)). The durable lesson: a level grid is a free soundness oracle, since levels are supposed to differ only in cost.

### Disjunction

Molly's lineage is a formula, disjunctive across alternative derivations of one fact, and its solver prunes fault sets that leave any alternative intact. Commit `73a5db9c05` recovered that behind `Ldfi::disjunctive_provenance` (`Prov` as `Leaf | All | Alts`, resolved lazily to a minimal DNF under `max_witnesses`). In three measurement passes it reduced hypotheses on no protocol in no cell. 2PC, ISR and Paxos exhibit no in-run alternatives; echo broadcast does, and there the count went up, 10 → 11 and 32 → 39 at `n=3`, +40% at `n=4` ([diagnostic](ldfi-level-ablation.md#diagnostic-why-disjunction-ran-more-hypotheses-on-broadcast)). The alternatives' deliveries add crash points the single path never names: saved 2 hypotheses, added 3. The structural reason is that hydrofrac's lazily grown universe already does most of Molly's pruning, since a fault is proposed only once some derivation depends on the send that names it ([disjunctive provenance](ldfi-level-ablation.md#disjunctive-provenance)). The flag stays off.

### Aggregate-local witnesses

Disjunction fired only at dedups, while the corpus's redundancy is quorum-shaped: a `fold` over `n` inputs and a threshold predicate, whose output is tagged with all `n`. Commit `2f156b6335` recovers "any `k` of `n`" behind `Ldfi::aggregate_witnesses`: a fold's cell becomes `Node::Agg`, and a downstream `filter` / `filter_map` re-folds every subset (up to `AGGREGATE_MAX_INPUTS`) in both orders, replacing the conjunction with the minimal passing subsets when the predicate proves upward-closed and order-independent on this run's inputs ([what was built](ldfi-level-ablation.md#what-was-built-1)). It fires on Paxos's P2b quorum (`collect_quorum`) and nowhere else: the P1b quorum selects responses by `anti_join`, through which provenance does not flow ([where it fires](ldfi-level-ablation.md#where-it-fires-in-the-corpus)). Paxos at cap 400 certified in 339 against `precise`'s 393, inside the ±50% draw variance `precise` shows across the document ([aggregate-local witnesses](ldfi-level-ablation.md#aggregate-local-counterfactual-witnesses)). Off by default; the conditions for retrying are listed under open doors.

### Fault point and fault model

The fault point is the sender because the first version put it at the consumer and paid for it. Round 1 used the simulator's scripted `BatchHook` with a standing `OmissionPolicy` on the receiving batch, with channels registered by the harness. A `tee`d channel omitted at one consumer was delivered at the others; hook-free paths could not be faulted; crashes needed a separate mechanism; every faultable batch was a parameter in the protocol's signature; every certificate rested on the author having registered every channel ([the redesign](history-hydrofrac-development.md#11-the-redesign-sender-side-filters)). A receiver-side test seam is not a model of the network. The filter on every `Network` send (`FaultState::filter` in `sim/runtime/lineage.rs`), with the transport type as the fault model, made every channel a fault point by construction ([the filter](history-hydrofrac-development.md#5-fault-injection-the-filter-on-every-send); [transports supply the fault model](hydrofrac.md#transports-supply-the-fault-model)).

### The specification is a Hydro flow

`Spec` builds an observer at a dedicated process and compiles it with the program. Observations arrive over an ordinary `fail_stop` channel and are presented in the observer's tick as the set of facts so far; views are bounded `Optional`s, and a view holds when it is non-empty at the end of the run, as a Datalog goal holds at fixpoint. The observer's outputs therefore have lineage like any other, and that lineage is what the search negates ([specifications as views](history-hydrofrac-development.md#65-specifications-pre-and-post-as-views)). Its ticks run eagerly, outside the schedule choice, because its interleaving is unobservable to the program. For `Spec::all_labels` the observer's input is filled by the `sim::observe` IR pass, which turns each labelled node into a `Tee` and ships the second branch to the observer before network compilation and the provenance rewrite, so taps are traced like any operator ([the specification is itself a Hydro flow](hydrofrac.md#the-specification-is-itself-a-hydro-flow)). A failure-free probe that yields nothing is `Outcome::Vacuous`, a verdict about the harness.

| provenance harnesses | compiled with provenance | `LDFI_NO_PROVENANCE=1` | the 2PC, ISR, broadcast and quorum harnesses skip `with_provenance()`, exercising replay-everywhere `Precise` |

`LDFI_PROV_DEBUG`, `LDFI_SOLVER_DEBUG`, `LDFI_LINEAGE_DEBUG` and `LDFI_REPLAY_DEBUG` print, respectively, records with more than one witness, every clause and hypothesis, the lineage log, and the replays. `.quiet()` silences the per-hypothesis progress line.

Three of these are numeric caps, each for a different reason. The hypothesis budget exists because the Molly loop has only two natural exits, an infeasible ILP (a certificate) or a violating hypothesis, and a lazily grown universe can keep adding crash points. The budget is the third exit, reported as `Outcome::Budget` rather than dressed as a certificate. It is a time bound, not a termination guarantee: the universe is finite (a bounded body sends finitely many messages, one crash per node, a no-good per tried set), so the ILP goes infeasible eventually. What hits the cap is slow or noisy: Raft at `Precise` runs out at 64 after 243–306 s; correct Paxos at one sampled schedule per hypothesis certifies at 203–230 in one pass and exhausts 256 in another, "a budget effect of the draw, not a verdict disagreement" ([core Paxos](ldfi-level-ablation.md#core-paxos-p2-a3-cr1-one-sampled-schedule-per-hypothesis-budget-256)).

The witness cap exists only because of disjunctive provenance: with several witnesses per record, derivations multiply as a cross product through conjunctions, and the ablation measured 4⁴ = 256 candidates for one goal on four-member echo broadcast. Over the cap, a conjunction falls back to the single union witness and a disjunction keeps its first `cap` alternatives; both are sound and counted. With disjunction off, as shipped, a cap of 1 reproduces the old single derivation bit for bit, so the number does not matter. `AGGREGATE_MAX_INPUTS` bounds the 2ⁿ re-folds, run twice, that test a fold's predicate for upward closure; past ten inputs the record keeps its conjunction. How the three values were chosen is settled below.

## Settled questions

### End of finite failures is part of the fault model, and is hand-placed

`EFF` is set by exactly one API, `Run::end_of_finite_failures()` in `ldfi.rs`, called from the test body. Its own doc comment calls it "Optional: a bound a body may impose on where omissions can fall". It has eight call sites, all in retrying or recovering protocols: `raft_ldfi.rs`, `paxos_ldfi.rs` (×2), `isr_replication.rs`, `ldfi_ablation_raft.rs` (×2), `ldfi_ablation_broadcast.rs`, `distributed/reliable_delivery.rs`. None of the `all_labels` EC harnesses (`ec_ldfi.rs`, `ec_demos_ldfi.rs`, `ec_all_labels_classes.rs`) call it; when it is not called, `Run::finish` sets `eff_cut = events.len()`, so EFF = EOT and the whole run is the failure window.

It is part of the fault model rather than a leftover because the filter and the fault universe are both defined relative to the cut and must agree. `FaultState::filter` in `sim/runtime/lineage.rs` gates both the `AfterSend` crash point and omissions on `!finite_failures_ended`. On the search side each send record carries `pre_eff: index < eff_cut`; `Dependency::candidate_faults` names crash points only by pre-EFF sends, with the ordinal clamped to the sender's pre-EFF count, and admits omission candidates only where `record.pre_eff && lossy`; `Dependency::killed_by` mirrors the filter; `all_pre_eff_deliveries` is the `Execution`-level derivation; `Schedule::pre_eff_sends` carries the counts into the hitting-set program. Commit `4ddea33f19` ("Molly fault model in the simulator", 2026-10-19) made the simulator side match, after a Raft search blamed a leaderless cluster for an entry it could never replicate ([end of finite failures and the horizon](history-hydrofrac-development.md#54-end-of-finite-failures-and-the-horizon)). The remark in [history §10](history-hydrofrac-development.md#10-limitations-and-what-is-not-built) that `EFF` "should not shape the design further" predates that commit and is superseded.

What remains open is that the search does not decide where failures stop; the harness author does, once per harness. A convergence witness in `PRE`, so that an unconverged run is `Vacuous` rather than a counterexample, is the alternative, and has no code behind it. Residual: whether Molly's own SAT encoding bounds crash times by EFF or by EOT should be checked against the paper's §3.

### The caps 256 / 8 / 10

None of the three was chosen by measurement. `max_hypotheses` defaults to 256 in `Ldfi::new`, verbatim from the first prototype (`b928c1fbd4`) with no stated rationale. It is under Raft's certificate (235) but over every Paxos certificate reported (301–568), and Paxos-correct hits `budget/256` at `Level::Execution` in the ablation, so harnesses override it: `ldfi_grid.rs` and `ldfi_ablation_two_pc.rs` use 400, `paxos_ldfi.rs` 2048. The recommendation is to raise the default to 1024, or have `Outcome::Budget` print the suggestion.

The 8 is two things. First, a harness constant `const SAMPLED: usize = 8` for `Exploration::Fuzz` in `isr_replication.rs`, `ldfi_ablation_isr.rs` and `quorum_ldfi.rs`, with the code-comment rationale that a hook-covered protocol's verdict does not depend on tick interleaving, so a sample suffices and the certificate is relative to it. Second, `DEFAULT_MAX_WITNESSES = 8` in `sim/runtime/provenance.rs` (from `73a5db9c05`), validated after the fact: two overflow sites across the whole ablation grid, first on a four-member broadcast where 4⁴ = 256 witnesses exceed it.

The 10 is `AGGREGATE_MAX_INPUTS` in `sim/runtime/provenance.rs` (from `2f156b6335`). Aggregate-local witnesses re-fold 2ⁿ subsets twice, so inputs are capped; the number is a complexity bound, not a measurement.

### Omission identity is `(channel, sender, destination, wire bytes)`

This was deliberate from the first implementation and has been reaffirmed since. The proposal ([message identity and time](ldfi-on-sim-hooks.md#message-identity-and-time)) specified `(channel, from, to, payload, k)` with occurrence counting, retransmissions as distinct faults. The same-day implementation (`b928c1fbd4`) shipped `Fault::Omit(Message)` as "discard every copy of this message on its channel" and listed occurrence counting under "Not done"; `ab3ad7fe82` fixed the identity as the `SendKey` in `sim/runtime/lineage.rs`. The reason ([the filter](history-hydrofrac-development.md#5-fault-injection-the-filter-on-every-send)): a fault must mean the same thing in every hypothesis even when the sender's send sequence shifts, the same ordinal-shift argument that renamed crash points from send counts to messages ([prototype status](ldfi-on-sim-hooks.md#prototype-status), soundness problem 1).

What is not expressible: "first copy lost, byte-identical retry delivered" within the failure window. A protocol whose retries must be distinguishable carries distinguishing bytes (`reliable_delivery.rs` `Msg { attempt }`, Raft's `leader_commit`). What is expressible: a byte-identical retry after `EFF` is delivered, since the filter checks `finite_failures_ended` first. Molly's omission is `omit(from, to, t)` on a logical clock, finer in time and coarser in payload, so Molly can express lost-then-delivered where hydrofrac cannot without a clock; the trade was made to keep fault identity stable without one.

### The old goal API is to be deleted

`Ldfi::goal`, `goal_all_members`, `goal_witnessed`, `Run::witness*` and `Run::require*` should be removed. `goal_all_members`, `goal_witnessed` and every `Run::witness*` / `require*` have zero callers in `hydro_test`, `hydro_lang` and `hydro_std`; `Ldfi::goal` has two (`reliable_delivery.rs`, `ldfi_ablation_broadcast.rs`), both via `Ldfi::run`, both expressible as a `Spec` with `observe(out)` and `post(.first())` and no `pre`. The API was introduced in `b928c1fbd4` (2026-10-09); the last `goal_all_members` harness went in `ab3ad7fe82` (2026-10-15); the last `goal_witnessed` / `require` harness (Raft) was replaced by `881605b6c0` (2026-10-16, "the old API remains available, unused"), which cut the Raft body from 60 to 25 lines and the hypothesis counts from 20 to 17 and 133 to 89.

No capability is lost. The one extra the old API offered, a Rust `bool` verdict from the body, is exactly the hazard [history §6.5](history-hydrofrac-development.md#65-specifications-pre-and-post-as-views) and [§10](history-hydrofrac-development.md#10-limitations-and-what-is-not-built) name: verdict and lineage can disagree, producing a wrong certificate with no warning. Deletion scope: `Goal::{Any, AllMembers, Witnessed}`; the `Run` fields `goal_port`, `witnesses`, `final_witnesses`, `requirements`; `Run::witness*` and `require*`; the helpers `any_output`, `all_members` and `witnessed*`; `Ldfi::goal*`; folding `Ldfi::run` into `search`. Keep the `Spec` `PRE` path (`spec_pre` feeding requirements), which is what `require` became. Update the `ldfi.rs` module docs §"Goals" and the one mention in hydrofrac.md §"Hand-written specifications". Not done in this branch.

## Closed doors

Nearly every closed door fell against one of four walls, and the walls are a better index than chronology.

*The wrong fault point.* A fault injected anywhere but the sender, or named by anything but the message, is not the fault Molly reasons about: its identity shifts between hypotheses, or it misses channels, or it explores a dimension the program cannot see.

*Unsound inference on a non-monotone view, or on an unsoundly attributed derivation.* Every false certificate came from here.

*The harness doing the protocol's job.* When the body arranges what the protocol should earn, the verdict is about the author.

*Finer provenance does not pay once the search universe is lazy.* Each finer account explains more exactly why a run succeeded; none changes which faults the search has reason to try, because a fault is proposed only when some derivation depends on the send that names it, and the coarse account already names those sends. Where the finer account named more sends, the universe grew and the search ran longer for a stronger certificate. The one place finer lineage paid, record provenance over execution lineage under load, is where the coarse account charged a record to deliveries it did not depend on, and that is the default.

Three doors fit none of the walls: two were about the scope of the claim, one about how it was measured.

## Doors never opened

- **Delay and reordering.** Expressible at the filter as hold-instead-of-drop; reordering is the schedule explorer's dimension ([history §5.6](history-hydrofrac-development.md#56-what-this-fault-point-cannot-do)).
- **Partitions as a primitive.** Omission sets already express them, and partitions miss selective-loss bugs ([history §9.1](history-hydrofrac-development.md#91-the-same-bug-under-maelstrom)).
- **Byzantine behaviour, duplication.** Outside Molly's vocabulary; duplication has no simulator hook ([limitations](hydrofrac.md#limitations)).
- **`cut_after(n)` on a `fail_stop` channel.** Nothing has needed a reliable channel to fail other than by its sender's crash ([history §5.6](history-hydrofrac-development.md#56-what-this-fault-point-cannot-do)).
- **The filter under `deploy`.** One OS-chosen interleaving per run, no determinism, replay or barriers: Molly's fixed schedule or worse ([history §11.3](history-hydrofrac-development.md#113-what-the-simulator-must-still-provide)).
- **A receive-side source for hook-free replay.** Would let `BatchHook` drop out of lineage entirely; deferred, since replay reproduces every hooked node and falls back soundly elsewhere ([history §11.3](history-hydrofrac-development.md#113-what-the-simulator-must-still-provide)).
- **Why-not provenance by Molly's De Morgan rewrite.** The simulator answers the counterfactual question without knowing how the view was computed ([negation §2](history-hydrofrac-negation.md#2-what-molly-did), [§3](history-hydrofrac-negation.md#3-lineage-as-intervention-on-nondeterministic-decisions)).
- **Exhaustive schedules for Raft; blind and random on the generic Raft body; a coverage-guided fuzzer baseline; Raft under load.** Each an hour to a day of compute not yet spent ([history §9](history-hydrofrac-development.md#9-what-it-has-found), [§10](history-hydrofrac-development.md#10-limitations-and-what-is-not-built)).
- **Raft `raft-bug` at `Precise` with a 256 cap in the shrunken ablation configuration**, to settle whether the L0 certificate on the bug variant there is a sampled-schedule miss ([Raft](ldfi-level-ablation.md#raft)).
- **The disjunction grid on 2PC `n ≥ 3` CTP, `n=4`, Paxos-correct and Raft**, lost to the out-of-memory reboot ([complete grid, partial](ldfi-level-ablation.md#complete-grid-2pc-paxos-leader_merge-disjunction--partial-salvaged)).

## Open doors

- **Scalar labels checked at their input stream.** A labelled `Singleton` / `Optional` / `KeyedSingleton` is a function of a stream the types already vouch for; the tap goes on the labelling operator's input and the verdict is reported under the scalar's site. ABD's register is the first case ([next steps](hydrofrac.md#next-steps)).
- **Convergence witnesses in `PRE`.** So an unconverged run is `Vacuous` rather than a counterexample; the Raft harness is the first candidate and needs the latest-heartbeat acknowledgement as an output ([next steps](hydrofrac.md#next-steps); [history §12](history-hydrofrac-development.md#12-where-it-stands-and-what-next)).
- **Decision-trace replay.** Record every nondeterministic decision and replay a prefix with one changed: the general oracle of which node-local replay is the coarse instance. It does not by itself repair the inference for non-monotone views ([negation §3](history-hydrofrac-negation.md#3-lineage-as-intervention-on-nondeterministic-decisions)).
- **Raft's `committed` under the generated specification.** The in-class form of the durability test, with the sound grade; about an hour per search, never run ([next steps](hydrofrac.md#next-steps)).
- **Membership change.** Nothing on this branch changes configuration while running; the shape is written down: agreement across an epoch boundary, a crash during reconfiguration, membership events as a driven input ([history §12](history-hydrofrac-development.md#12-where-it-stands-and-what-next)).
- **Legible Lamport diagrams for wide protocols.** `sim::viz::lamport` spaces lanes by the longest message label, so a seven-node multi-Paxos counterexample is ~36 inches wide and unreadable on a page. Payloads should be abbreviated (an index into a legend, or a hash) so lane spacing follows node count ([molly-viz.md](molly-viz.md#limits)).
- **DNF cover constraints over the single-derivation universe.** Keep every alternative as a clause but propose crash points only from first-witness derivations, dropping any clause the universe cannot hit; the one disjunction experiment left ([status](ldfi-level-ablation.md#status)).
- **Replay cost on long runs.** Cache replays across hypotheses whose scripts share a prefix; a Raft hypothesis late in the certificate search takes three to four minutes, almost all replay ([history §12](history-hydrofrac-development.md#12-where-it-stands-and-what-next)).
- **Deleting the old goal API.** Settled above; the deletion itself is not done.
- **Raising the default hypothesis budget.** Settled above (1024, or a printed suggestion under `Outcome::Budget`); not done.
- **Aggregate witnesses, retried under the right conditions.** A quorum predicate on the records' path (a count, not an anti-join of responses), alternatives in the failure-free run, a search long enough for a smaller clause to matter, and a multi-pass measurement over several sampled schedules.
