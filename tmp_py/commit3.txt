fix(hydrofrac): tag order-dependent top-level folds and scans as fragile

## The bug: a false certificate
`provenance/fragile.rs` tagged `fold`, `fold_keyed`, `reduce`, `reduce_keyed`
and `scan` as fragile only inside a tick. But a top-level `TotalOrder` stream
can carry an order the simulator chooses: `assume_ordering` at top level
(`ObserveNonDet` with `trusted: false`) compiles to a
`TopLevelStreamOrderHook` (`hydro_lang/src/sim/builder.rs`), which is a
schedule choice. A top-level `scan` after it depends on that order, but was
untagged. The program counted as fragile-free, ran on one schedule, and was
certified.

Repro, now the regression test
`check::brute_tests::a_top_level_scan_over_a_chosen_order_is_fragile`:
- two processes each `broadcast_closed` one value (1 and 2) to two members;
- each member merges them, applies `assume_ordering::<TotalOrder>`, then
  `scan`s to keep the first value;
- the output is labelled eventually consistent.

Before the fix, `fragile_sites() == Some([])` and the search said "holds with
up to 0 crashes", while brute force broke the label with the empty set. Now
`scan` is a fragile site, and the search finds the no-fault counterexample,
in agreement with brute force.

## The fix: an allowlist
`View::fragile` is now an allowlist. An operator is robust only on an arm
that says why it is insensitive to arrival order and batching:
- plumbing;
- element-wise operators with pure closures;
- set-preserving merges and reorderings;
- networks, and `batch` of a stream;
- fixed sources and the harness's inputs;
- top-level joins (with `JoinHalf`'s bounded build side complete before it is
  probed);
- the dedup idiom;
- constant inputs.

Every other kind is fragile through a default arm. That includes folds,
reduces and scans anywhere, snapshots, negation, `cross_singleton`,
`enumerate`, `chain_first`, and any IR kind added later. Opaque islands keep
no tag, since lineage already treats their records as fragile.

Sources decide directly: a source has no inputs, so the constant-inputs
exemption would hold for it vacuously.

## Gates (every label of every check, before vs after)
- `cargo test -p hydrofrac --features cross-check`: 141 passed (140 + the
  regression).
- `cargo test -p hydro_std --lib hydrofrac`: 12 passed.
- `cargo test -p hydro_test --lib cluster::ec_`: 42 passed.
- No verdict changed and no certificate was lost. The only cost changes are
  in sampled searches (multi-Paxos, uniform broadcast and CTP-3 demos, which
  sample 8 or 64 schedules), i.e. sampling noise.
