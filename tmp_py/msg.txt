wip: test(hydrofrac): a second, independent cross-check oracle

Oracle 2 (`hydrofrac/src/check/oracle2.rs`, `cfg(any(test, feature = "cross-check"))`) runs after the
search and the brute force in every `EcCheck::cross_check`, and adds its disagreements (prefixed
`oracle 2:`) to `CrossCheck::disagreements`; `CrossCheck`'s `Display` prints its summary.

## What it does not share with the search

- **Its own program**: `EcCheck::instrument` forks the incoming flow with the new
  `SimFlow::try_fork` (`hydro_lang/src/sim/flow.rs`: deep-clones the IR, gives the copy fresh port
  counters and a fresh external-port registry, clones every other field; returns `None` when IR
  passes are registered, as `FnOnce` closures cannot be copied). Oracle 2 compiles the fork lazily,
  with hook points, but **without** the provenance rewrite and without hydrofrac's tap pass.
- **Its own taps**: one external output per labelled cluster node where the label is minted (no
  labelled input) or exits (an unlabelled non-send consumer, or a program output), by node identity.
  Elements leave as `Option<bincode>` (`None` when not `Serialize`: then not compared). Scalars and
  in-tick nodes are listed as skipped.
- **Its own faults and hook**: omission of a message by content on a lossy channel; crash before the
  first send or right after the node's `k`-th send; only before the heal (`body; quiesce; heal;
  quiesce`, as the brute force drives it).
- **Its own enumeration** (rewritten, mirroring the brute force's fixpoint over a harvested
  universe) and the same bounds (one crash per node, `max_crashes`, never every node, at most
  `|members| - 2` of the cluster).
- **Its own comparison**: per schedule and collection, correct members' element sets must be equal;
  a panic breaks everything. No lineage, no `spec.rs`, no pruning.

It still shares the simulator, the user's IR, the harness, and (for mapping collections to labels
only) `ec_label` and the `(label, operator, kind, cluster)` key.

## Agreement rules

- a claim of holding with up to `k` crashes is refuted by an oracle-2 breaking set with at most `k`
  crashes;
- a counterexample of `n <= max_faults` faults and `c` crashes (unsampled) needs an oracle-2
  breaking set with at most `n` faults and `c` crashes;
- the failure-free runs agree on vacuity (empty domain).

## Limitations (documented in the module)

- one collection per node: stricter than the search's union per label;
- crash after a re-sent payload's second copy is outside the search's vocabulary;
- non-`Serialize` elements, scalars, in-tick nodes are not compared.
